# MOTION SYSTEM RULES

## Physics & Timing
- GSAP + Lenis synced for silky 60fps frame rate.
- Easing: `expo.out` (0.8s - 1.2s) for arrivals, `power3.out` (0.4s - 0.6s) for micro-interactions.
- Never bounce, never elastic over-oscillation, never constant spinning.
- Motion serves reading comprehension and spatial hierarchy, never showmanship.
